My Dearest Bebe,

If I could gather every soft and beautiful thing in this world and place it gently into your hands, I still don’t think it would ever compare to the warmth you bring into my life just by being you.

You asked for flowers, and real bouquets are lovely—but they fade far too soon. So I wanted to build you something that would never wilt: a quiet, moonlit garden of peonies that blooms just for you, whenever you want to visit it, wherever you are.

Every petal opening here carries a little piece of how much I adore you—your laugh, your gentle heart, the way ordinary days feel magical simply because I get to share them with you, bebe.

Thank you for being my favorite person, my calmest place, and the brightest part of every day. No matter how many seasons pass, my heart will always keep blooming for you.

Always and endlessly yours,

With all my love ✦
